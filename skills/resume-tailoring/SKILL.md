# Resume Tailoring Procedure

## Trigger / when to use
Load when applying to a specific role and you have the job description — the resume must be re-pointed at that posting, not sent generic.

## Approach
1. **Extract the job's vocabulary**: list the required skills, tools, and verbs from the posting. The resume must use the posting's exact terms (ATS matches on strings, not concepts).
2. **Reorder, don't inflate**: move the most relevant experience to the top of each role. Cut anything that doesn't serve this posting — a targeted 1-page resume beats a generic 2-pager.
3. **Write impact bullets with numbers**: "Improved X by Y% by doing Z" — outcome first, method second, metric always. No numbers? Use scope (users, revenue, latency, team size).
4. **Match the format to the role**: engineering roles want depth (projects, stack, scale); leadership wants breadth and outcomes. One resume is not all jobs.
5. **Stay honest**: every claim must survive a 5-minute interview question. Exaggeration that collapses in screening is worse than a weaker true claim.

## Pitfalls
- Keyword-stuffing that reads as spam to a human screener.
- Listing every technology ever touched instead of the ones this job needs.
- "Responsible for" (task) vs. "Delivered" (outcome) — always the latter.

## Verification
A stranger can point to the bullet that proves each requirement in the job description; every bullet has a metric or scope; the resume is one page for <10 years experience.
Check the resume against the job description line by line — each requirement maps to at least one bullet.
